01 / ABOUT

What is CFB?

CFB — Chandan Foundation Batch — is an initiative built around one belief: strong outcomes come from strong foundations. We work with students to strengthen core concepts, build durable knowledge, and develop the practical skills that carry them from the classroom into their academic and future goals.

Every layer of learning at CFB is built on the one before it — fundamentals first, then guided practice, then mastery, then the confidence to lead. It's a structured path, not a shortcut.

STRUCTURED · DELIBERATE · STUDENT-FIRST
